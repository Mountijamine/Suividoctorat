Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started

How long does it take to set up?

You can create your account and start managing your research in under 5 minutes. Our AI-assisted onboarding makes it incredibly fast.

Is my research data secure?

Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade encryption, comply with GDPR, and store all data on secure servers with regular backups.

Can I collaborate with my advisors?

Yes! Invite unlimited advisors and committee members. They get free access to collaborate on your research projects.

What file formats are supported?

We support all major formats including PDF, Word, LaTeX, Excel, and can integrate with reference managers like Zotero and Mendeley.

Is there a mobile app?

Our platform is fully responsive and works perfectly on mobile. Native iOS and Android apps are coming in Q2 2025.

What if I need help?

We offer 24/7 support via chat, email, and video calls. Plus, access to our knowledge base and community forum anytime.
